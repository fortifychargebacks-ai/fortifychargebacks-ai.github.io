Knowledge Base

Timeframes &
reason codes.

Missed deadlines mean lost revenue. Understanding filing windows and reason codes for each card network is critical.

Dispute Timeframes

Standard: 120 Days
Code Description Limit Notes
10.4 Other Fraud — Card-Absent Environment 120 Days From transaction date. Common for ecommerce.
13.1 Merchandise/Services Not Received 120 Days From expected delivery date.
13.3 Not as Described or Defective 120 Days Customer must attempt return first.
11.3 No Authorization 75 Days Shorter timeframe. Processing errors.
* Timeframes are general guidelines. Specific issuer rules may vary. Always respond within 24–48 hours.

Dispute Timeframes

Standard: 120 Days
Code Description Limit Notes
4837 No Cardholder Authorization (Fraud) 120 Days From transaction settlement date.
4853 Cardholder Dispute (Defective/Not Received) 120 Days From delivery or expected service date.
4808 Authorization Related Chargeback 90 Days Strict timeframe. Proof of valid auth required.
4834 Point of Interaction Error 120 Days Includes duplicate processing or late presentment.

Dispute Timeframes

Inquiry Reply: 20 Days
Code Description Reply Limit Notes
F29 Fraud — Card Not Present 20 Days Immediate reply. Verify AVS/CVV matches.
C08 Goods/Services Not Received 20 Days Provide tracking to AVS address.
C31 Goods/Services Not As Described 20 Days Expert opinion often strengthens case.
M01 No Authorization 20 Days Processed without valid approval code.
* Amex typically requires a response within 20 days to avoid immediate chargeback.

PULSE / Discover

Varies by Reg E

Regulation E Guidelines

PULSE disputes often fall under US Regulation E (Electronic Fund Transfer Act).

  • Consumers generally have 60 days from the statement date to dispute.
  • Merchants should retain documentation for at least 2 years.

Common PULSE Codes

UA01 Fraud / Not Authorized
IN01 Item Not Received
DP01 Duplicate Processing
