Merchant Awareness

Chargeback Timeframes & Codes

Understanding the filing deadlines and reason codes for each card association is critical for your defense. Missed deadlines mean lost revenue.

Dispute Timeframes

Standard Limit: 120 Days
Reason Code Description Time Limit (Days) Notes
10.4 Other Fraud - Card-Absent Environment 120 Days Counted from transaction date. Common for ecommerce.
13.1 Merchandise/Services Not Received 120 Days Counted from the expected delivery date.
13.3 Not as Described or Defective 120 Days Customer must attempt to return goods first.
11.3 No Authorization 75 Days Shorter timeframe. Primarily for processing errors.
* Timeframes are general guidelines. Specific issuer rules may vary. Always respond to us within 24-48 hours.

Timeframes

Standard Limit: 120 Days
Reason Code Description Time Limit (Days) Notes
4837 No Cardholder Authorization (Fraud) 120 Days From transaction settlement date.
4853 Cardholder Dispute (Defective/Not Received) 120 Days From delivery date or date services were expected.
4808 Authorization Related Chargeback 90 Days Strict timeframe. Requires proof of valid authorization.
4834 Point of Interaction Error 120 Days Includes duplicate processing or late presentment.

Dispute Codes

Inquiry Reply: 20 Days
Reason Code Description Reply Limit Notes
F29 Fraud - Card Not Present 20 Days Immediate reply required. Verify AVS/CVV matches.
C08 Goods/Services Not Received 20 Days Provide tracking proof showing delivery to AVS address.
C31 Goods/Services Not As Described 20 Days Written opinion from a qualified expert often helps.
M01 No Authorization 20 Days Charge was processed without valid approval code.
* Amex typically requires a response to an inquiry within 20 days to avoid an immediate chargeback.

PULSE / Discover

Varies by Reg E

Regulation E Guidelines

PULSE disputes often fall under US Regulation E (Electronic Fund Transfer Act).

  • Consumers generally have 60 days from the date of the periodic statement containing the error to dispute it.
  • Merchants should retain documentation for at least 2 years.

Common PULSE Codes

UA01 Fraud / Not Authorized
IN01 Item Not Received
DP01 Duplicate Processing
